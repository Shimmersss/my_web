# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

**中文** · [英文](#english)

## 中文

本项目采用 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 作为行为准则。

### 我们的承诺

无论年龄、体型、是否残障、族裔、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向如何，我们都致力于让每个人在参与本社区时免受骚扰。

### 我们期望的行为

- 尊重不同的观点与经验，友善、耐心地交流；
- 给出并虚心接受建设性的反馈；
- 为自己的错误负责、道歉并从中学习；
- 关注对整个社区最有益的事情。

### 不可接受的行为

- 使用带有性暗示的语言或图像，或进行不受欢迎的性关注；
- 挑衅、侮辱或贬损性评论，人身或政治攻击；
- 公开或私下骚扰；
- 未经明确许可公开他人的私人信息（如住址、邮箱）；
- 其他在专业场合中会被合理认为不当的行为。

### 适用范围与执行

本准则适用于所有项目空间（Issue、PR、讨论区等），以及代表本项目的公开场合。违规行为可通过 [GitHub 私信或私密报告](https://github.com/Shimmersss/my_web/security/advisories/new)告知维护者。维护者会保密处理，并可视情况采取提醒、警告、临时或永久禁止参与等措施。

## English

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

### Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

### Expected behaviour

- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences; communicate kindly and patiently.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community as a whole.

### Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention;
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- Public or private harassment;
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission;
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

### Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, …) and when representing the project in public. Report violations to the maintainers through [a private report on GitHub](https://github.com/Shimmersss/my_web/security/advisories/new). Reports are handled confidentially; maintainers may respond with a reminder, a warning, or a temporary or permanent ban.
