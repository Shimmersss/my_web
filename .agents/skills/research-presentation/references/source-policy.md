# Source policy

- Rank sources: primary research or official publication; standards and government; first-party organization; reputable secondary analysis.
- Treat search snippets as discovery only. Use returned metadata or extracted page content for claims.
- Use `sourceIds` on the slide that contains the claim. A final bibliography does not replace slide-level attribution.
- Keep quotations short and prefer paraphrase.
- Discard sources without a stable URL or enough metadata to identify them.
- Record an image only when its origin and reuse status are known.
- When sources disagree, represent the disagreement instead of selecting a convenient number.
